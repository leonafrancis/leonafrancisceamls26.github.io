what_i_learned: |
  We spent the entire morning preparing for our practice presentation session with Dr. Mack. Based on our morning review, we adjusted a few final details in our deck before heading over to present. When it was our turn to step up, my nerves definitely kicked in—I was so anxious I could barely stay still! However, I pushed through and made a conscious, concentrated effort not to read directly off the slides while speaking.
During the feedback portion, Dr. Mack and the reviewers shared that our PowerPoint looked great and loved that we customized the design to make it uniquely our own rather than strictly sticking to the standard template. Best of all, Dr. Mack told me directly that I did way better presenting today than I did during the mid-summer symposium! The remaining critiques were just minor details to tweak rather than major structural changes.
After all the presentation practice wrapped up, we got to unwind with our long-awaited tie-dye session! We finally got to dye our pink team shirts.
blockers: |
  My biggest blocker today was once again managing my intense stage fright. My anxiety made it really hard to stand still at the podium, and trying to stay calm while mentally tracking my talking points and avoiding reading the slides took every ounce of focus I had.

reflection: |
  Hearing Dr. Mack say that I improved so much since my last presentation made me happy! Public speaking has been my biggest struggle throughout this entire program, so getting validation that my hard work and practice are paying off feels incredible. Knowing that the reviewers loved our custom slide design and that we only have minor tweaks left gives me so much relief.
To top it all off, the tie-dye session was so much fun and the absolute best way to decompress after such a nerve-wracking morning. I can’t wait to see how my shirt officially turns out once it sets and dries! I'm walking away today with a real sense of pride, great memories with my team, and way more confidence for the final presentation.
