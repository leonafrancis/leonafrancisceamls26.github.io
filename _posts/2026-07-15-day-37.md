what_i_learned: |
  Today was all about post-production and polishing our final elevator pitch video. We spent the day combining our dramatic acted footage with the technical side of our project. I learned how to sync a recorded voiceover with a screen recording of our app's front-end interface, ensuring the audio explanation perfectly matched the visual transition on screen. We focused on accessibility by manually adding captions across the entire video so it is easy to follow, and mixed in some subtle background music, learning how to balance the audio levels so the track adds energy without overpowering our voices. After, we went to the SAIRI Connect session. It was nice to take a break and bond with the wider group. We learned about the upcoming activities, including the field day and the day where we get to tie-dye our team shirts! 

blockers: |
  Our biggest technical hurdle today was dealing with visual effects. We tried to add in some creative transitions and video effects to make the video pop, but we realized they just didn't look professional enough. We had to make the executive decision to scrap those effects entirely and stick to clean, simple cuts to preserve a highly professional aesthetic. On the social side, securing my team shirt color(pink the best choice) took some serious politicking! At first we were going to be yellow however, I heard someone mention pink and immediately decided that we had to have pink. I had to convince my teammates and then we had to make sure pink was an option. We got it in the end though so thats all that matters. 

reflection: |
  It feels amazing to finally have a finished, fully rendered video! Even though we had to spend a lot of time stripping out the tacky video effects, leaning into a cleaner design actually made our project stand out much more. On a personal note, seeing myself on the video made me cringe so bad! The video itself is very cute, but omg... my acting. Every single time I'm on camera or speaking in the clip, I literally have to turn my head away because I'm too embarrassed to watch. Despite the camera shyness, I am absolutely overjoyed that my team successfully secured the color pink for our shirts after all that debate! I cannot wait until we get to tie-dye them. The upcoming games sound incredibly fun, and I'm genuinely excited to see how everything plays out and who takes home the win that day!
