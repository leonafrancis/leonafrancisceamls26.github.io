  We started the morning with some quick, high-energy games since we only had a limited window before our guest lecture. I learned the mechanics of how computers can interface with brain activity and got to see how BCIs are actively being applied in real-world technology and medicine today. Following the lecture, we joined the rest of the cohorts on zoom to screen everyone's 90-second elevator pitch videos. Seeing the sheer creativity and humor of the other teams was amazing—Group 5 and Group 7 were definitely in my top three (right after our own group's video, of course!). We also spent time polishing the front-end UI of our app to make it look much cuter and more visually appealing!
