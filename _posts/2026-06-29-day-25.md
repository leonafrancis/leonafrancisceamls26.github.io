what_i_learned: |
  Today was the day of the mid-summer symposium. We spent the early morning running through the project together as a group one last time to prepare. When we hit our morning break, we found out our group was scheduled to present before lunch instead of after, which completely threw off my timeline and sent my anxiety into overdrive. Despite the intense stage fright, we got up there and presented our project(we had no choice). After lunch, the day shifted into a listening session. I learned a ton by watching the other research groups present. It was fascinating to see how far everyone has come, to compare our progress with theirs, and to learn about the specific technical roadblocks they faced and the ways they overcame them.
blockers: |
  My biggest blocker today wasn't the code or the slides, it was my own nerves. I woke up late, which got the morning off to a rough, rushed start. I tried practicing my slides and my groupmates did everything they could to calm me down, but nothing worked. The anxiety was so overwhelming that I felt physically sick(I was literally wiping my tears, I wanted to cry) the entire time we were standing up there presenting. My intense dislike for public speaking made me want to just run off the stage and hide in the bathroom. 
reflection: |
  If I am being totally honest, I would give today a 0/10 because I absolutely hate speaking out loud in front of crowds. I was incredibly glad when it was over and we finally got to go to lunch, where I could relax, enjoy the food, and chat with everyone without the dark cloud of the presentation hanging over me. While I loved seeing the amazing work the other groups produced, I am dreading the final symposium. Just thinking about it makes me anxious because I know the pressure will feel even higher, but I'm glad this is at least behind us.
