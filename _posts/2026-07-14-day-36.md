what_i_learned: |
  Today was a massive milestone day! We started by  editing our research paper to make sure it was flawless before submitting it today. Afterward, we immediately shifted into creative production mode for our 90-second elevator pitch video. We learned what goes into independent filmmaking: scouting a good location, managing a timeline, and collecting a bunch of specific props to set the scene, including a lab coat, a magnifying glass, markers, and printed pictures. We spent two hours filming multiple takes to make sure the acting, framing, and delivery met our standards. Afterward, we spent an hour in the editing bay clipping scenes together and adding background music. Our final cut is the perfect mix of cute, dramatic, and highly informative! Tomorrow, we just need to insert our app's screen recording and sync up the final voiceover. 

blockers: |
  The biggest blocker today was the editing process specifically the time management. Our footage was so fun and dramatic that trying to edit the sequence down to stay strictly under the 90-second limit was incredibly difficult. It’s a tight squeeze because we have to leave enough precious seconds in the runtime to showcase the screen recording of our actual working app without rushing the explanation.

reflection: |
  Submitting the paper today felt like a massive weight was lifted off our shoulders! Turning our attention to the video pitch was the ultimate creative outlet to celebrate. I absolutely love how our video turned out; we leaned into the drama of medical research while keeping it educational, and it's honestly so cute. Filming for two hours was exhausting, but collaborating on something so lighthearted with my groupmates made it one of the best days of the program so far. I can't wait to wrap up the voiceover tomorrow and see the final product even if I cringe watching myself!
