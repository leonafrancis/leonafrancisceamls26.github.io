I decided to choose the Google Machine Learning class Neural Networks to expand my knowledge in deep learning. The most significant technical difficulty faced on that day was trying to implement Individual Typology Angle (ITA) algorithm in relation to our primary project's data set. ITA is a metric that helps classify the skin color based on its value in digital photos and is based on conversion of RGB pixels' values to CIELAB color space and certain mathematical formula of arc-tangent.
