what_i_learned: |
  Today was entirely dedicated to polishing and perfecting our final symposium slides. Building on yesterday's layout assignments, we spent the whole day editing our slides to strike the perfect balance between high-level visual appeal, readability, and technical detail. We focused on ensuring that complex information like our model's performance metrics and the Individual Typology Angle (ITA) calculations, was presented clearly without cluttering the screen. During the afternoon, our mentor Blessing reviewed our progress and gave us direct, constructive feedback on our design choices and content flow. We spent the remainder of the session implementing her suggestions, sharpening our headlines, adjusting font sizes for better visual hierarchy, and ensuring every slide directly supported our core research narrative. 

blockers: |
  Finding the sweet spot between concise bullet points and comprehensive data was our biggest hurdle today. When you've put so much effort into the underlying machine learning model and paper, it’s tempting to put every detail on the screen. It took several rounds of revisions and feedback from Blessing to trim down dense paragraphs into clean, easily digestible takeaways that won't overwhelm the audience during our talk.

reflection: |
  Getting feedback from Blessing helped us see our presentation through the audience's eyes, and incorporating her notes made a huge difference in the overall quality of the deck. Even though continuous editing can be repetitive, seeing our slides look so sleek, professional, and well-structured gives me a lot more confidence as we head closer to presentation day!
