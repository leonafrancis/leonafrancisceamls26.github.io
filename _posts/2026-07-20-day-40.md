what_i_learned: |
  Today marked the official beginning of our preparation for the final symposium presentation. We kicked off the day by organizing our slideshow strategy and dividing up the workload fairly across the team, assigning each group member slides to work on and design. My assigned set required me to dive back into our final research paper and our original project plan to pull out the most critical data and technical explanations. I had to dissect our dense academic paper and translate detailed methodology into clear, scannable bullet points for our presentation. A big chunk of my focus went toward visual accessibility—making sure the words was large enough, and the formatting was easy to read from a distance so the audience can see the information effortlessly while we speak. 

blockers: |
  The biggest challenge today was deciding what information to keep and what to leave out. Squeezing 9-weeks of research from our paper onto our slides is tough. It took a lot of careful editing to ensure my slides were thoroughly explained without turning them into overwhelming walls of text. 

reflection: |
  Even though public speaking makes me nervous, taking the time to carefully build our slides made me happy as it marks the end of this program. Dissecting our paper forced me to revisit all the hard work we’ve put in over the past few weeks, and it’s amazing to see how far our project has come. Im excited and a little nervous for the symposium! Im ready to close out the program and see the final results of all the projects.
