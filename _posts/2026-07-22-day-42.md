what_i_learned: |
  Today was entirely focused on mastering our final presentation and putting the finishing touches on our slide deck before submitting them. We spent the day reviewing every single slide, making sure the fonts were large and legible, trimming down wordy bullet points, and ensuring the overall design was clean and uniform across the deck. Once the formatting was set, we transitioned into practice mode. We ran through our entire presentation multiple times to build up muscle memory, get comfortable with our speaking transitions, and make sure we really knew our material inside and out. During each run-through, our mentor Blessing threw practice questions at us to simulate the Q&A portion of the symposium, forcing us to think on our feet and articulate our technical choices clearly.

blockers: |
  Rehearsing repeatedly while simultaneously making micro-adjustments to the slides can be exhausting. It was a constant cycle of speaking, catching a slide that felt too crowded or hard to read, pausing to edit the layout, and then restarting the run-through. Dealing with Blessing’s surprise practice questions during the rehearsals was also a bit nerve-wracking, but it quickly highlighted which parts of our explanation needed more clarity.

reflection: |
  All the continuous practice today really paid off! Handling Blessing's Q&A sessions during our trial runs made me realize just how much deeper my understanding of our research has become since the mid-summer symposium. Fine-tuning the slides until they looked totally uniform gave our whole group a huge boost of confidence. Submitting our final deck at the end of the day feels like a major accomplishment, and I'm feeling much more prepared to step up and deliver a great presentation!
