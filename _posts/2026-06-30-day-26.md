what_i_learned: |
  Today was our field trip to the AWS Summit in Washington, D.C., and it was an incredible experience packed with learning and networking. I spent the day jumping in and out of different breakout rooms and workshops. First, I attended a session titled "Outpace the Adversary: Cyber Defense at AI Speed" (hosted by Booz Allen), which opened my eyes to how artificial intelligence is transforming modern cybersecurity. I also went to a builder's guide session called "Protecting the data behind your AI" (hosted by Commvault), which taught me about the data infrastructure required to keep AI systems secure. On the hands-on side, I participated in a workshop titled "Cloud.gov Workshop: Compliant Software Delivery in the AI Agent era" (hosted by GitLab). I even jumped into another technical workshop where we got to build a machine learning system that categorized flight records! Beyond the sessions, I spent a lot of time networking. I met tons of industry professionals and even connected with a fellow Morgan State University student who is currently interning at CGI.
blockers: |
  The summit was absolutely massive, and there was so much information being thrown at us all at once. By the end of the day, my brain was completely fried from information overload—to the point where I completely forgot the exact name of the flight-record machine learning workshop I attended!(I didnt even get a picture...) It was also a bit overwhelming trying to navigate the crowded expo floors and choose between so many overlapping sessions.
reflection: |
  After the stress of the mid-summer symposium, today was exactly the kind of reset I needed. I had an amazing time building my professional networ, and seeing how the AI concepts we are practicing in our lab apply to massive corporations and government security. Meeting other students interning at major companies like CGI was incredibly motivating. Today made me realize just how big the tech world is and how many opportunities are out there waiting for us!
