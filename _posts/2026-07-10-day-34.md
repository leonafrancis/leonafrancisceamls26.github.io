what_i_learned: |
  We wrapped up the week by hitting two major development milestones for our project. First, my group and I put the finishing touches on our slideshow for the upcoming weekly presentation, ensuring all our latest model updates and methodologies were perfectly documented. With the slides out of the way, we shifted our full focus to our application's development. We spent the rest of the day grinding on the codebase and successfully got our front-end interface up and running! To make the app as intuitive as possible for first-time users, we designed and built descriptive information slide pages right onto the welcome screen. Now, when a user downloads and opens the app for the very first time, they are greeted by a clean, multi-page introduction carousel. I learned how to structure this type of onboarding flow to introduce users to our core features—specifically explaining how the app utilizes AI to estimate skin tone equity and analyze skin lesions. This layout ensures that our complex machine learning research is broken down into friendly, bite-sized highlights before the user even reaches the main dashboard. 

blockers: |
  While the front end is looking great and going smoothly, our biggest hurdle right now is the back end. We still have a lot of work to do to connect our user interface to our trained machine learning model behind the scenes. Getting the pipeline to seamlessly pass user images through our skin tone estimation and skin cancer detection models without crashing is proving to be a complex integration puzzle. Additionally, we had to find the perfect balance between keeping the introductory slides visually appealing and technically informative without cluttering the screen. Since our app deals with sensitive topics, we had to carefully phrase our feature descriptions so they are accessible to anyone. 

reflection: |
  It feels amazing to end the week seeing a tangible app on the screen instead of just lines of code in a notebook! Adding these welcome slides makes our project feel less like a student experiment and much more like a real, market-ready mobile app. Even though knowing the back end still needs heavy development is a bit daunting, seeing the front end work properly is a massive morale booster. Watching our frontend evolve from a simple interface into a thoughtful, guided user journey is incredibly rewarding, and having our weekly presentation deck fully polished also takes a huge weight off our shoulders. I'm glad we can head into the weekend with some solid momentum!
