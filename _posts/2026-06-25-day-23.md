what_i_learned: |
  Today was all about teamwork, designing, and public speaking as we worked on our slides in preparation for our mid-summer symposium. In the morning session, we worked hard on designing the rest of the slides that we hadn't done in the previous class. After the lunch break, we got together to go through all of our slides in one piece, making sure that the visual elements were consistent and the storyline flowed smoothly. In the last part of the session, we practiced our presentation skills as we presented our slides. With the valuable feedback from our mentor, Blessing, we gained even more insight about how to present ourselves confidently. She also quizzed us with some mock practice questions to prepare us for what the judges might ask us during the symposium, and this helped me appreciate the amount that I have learned about our model of detecting skin cancer.
blockers: |
  Combining different peoples' presentations into one is never easy. After lunch, it took us quite some time to adjust the fonts, the design of each slide and the transition from one slide to another so that the whole presentation appears as an effort by the whole group and not just random slides stuck together.
reflection: |
  It feels great now that I have gained confidence through the practice session today! Though it was quite nervous being asked unexpected questions by Blessing, it was precisely what we needed for us to become familiar with the topic. Now that our individual slides have all merged into a perfect slide presentation, I feel really proud of how much my lab has been able to accomplish thus far.
