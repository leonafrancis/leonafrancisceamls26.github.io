what_i_learned: |
  We spent the morning doing a final run-through of our presentation slides to make sure our talking points were completely locked in. Afterward, we met up with the rest of the cohort to do a walkthrough of the actual room where we will be delivering our final presentations. While checking out the room layout, we went over the full schedule and logistics for both Saturday and Sunday so everyone knows exactly where they need to be and when. Following lunch, we transitioned to a well-deserved social event: the program Field Day! While I chose to sit out of the physical events and stay on the sidelines as a spectator, I learned a lot about sports photography and video capturing as I documented the entire event for our group, capturing tons of hilarious moments and photos of everyone competing.
blockers: |
  Seeing the presentation room for the first time gave me a massive surge of anxiety. I realized there aren't going to be any confidence monitors facing the podium for me to look at if I accidentally forget my place or freeze up on a slide. Knowing I won't have that visual safety net right in front of me is definitely scary, but it just means I have to rely on my preparation and memory even more.

reflection: |
  Despite the venue scare this morning, today was a great mix of serious preparation and fun. Field Day was so funny to watch from the sidelines, and taking pictures and videos of my cohort messing around was the perfect way to distract myself from presentation nerves. Stepping into the actual presentation room made everything feel real, and while not having confidence monitors is intimidating, I know how hard I've practiced. I'm ready to push through the stage fright and give it my all this weekend!
